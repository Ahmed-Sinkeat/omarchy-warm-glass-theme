# Wallpapers

These 18 images are the user's selected wallpaper rotation, re-encoded to WebP (max 4K wide) from their collection.
Matching filenames appear in [orangc's wallpaper collection](https://github.com/orangci/walls)
and its [Catppuccin Mocha version](https://github.com/orangci/walls-catppuccin-mocha).
The numeric filename prefixes preserve the selected order.

The artworks belong to their respective creators. The theme's MIT license does not cover
these wallpapers. Individual artists and redistribution terms have not been verified;
the linked collections do not establish ownership or permission for each image.

If you are a creator who wants attribution corrected or an image removed, please
[open an issue](https://github.com/Ahmed-Sinkeat/omarchy-warm-glass-theme/issues).
